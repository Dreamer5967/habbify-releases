# 📜 Changelog

All notable changes to the **Habbify** project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [v1.0.0] - 2026-08-12

### 🎉 Initial Official Release (v1.0.0)

#### 🏋️ Gym Planner & Muscle Map
- Added interactive color-indexed anatomical muscle diagram (Front & Back views).
- Added **Forearms** muscle group and forearm exercise routines.
- Sampling exact color codes for all 10 muscle groups (Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Forearms, Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves, Core).
- Added Personal Record (PR) weight tracking system.
- Pre-loaded 60+ exercises with equipment & target muscle filters.

#### 🎯 Habit & Routine Tracking
- 52-week contribution heatmaps (GitHub-style).
- Streak counting and level/XP reward system.
- Daily routine checklist with completion history.

#### 🧠 Focus & Productivity
- Pomodoro focus timer with circular SVG progress visualizer.
- Ambient sounds, customizable work/break durations, and automated XP rewards.

#### 💰 Finance & Goal Tracking
- Budget logging with income vs. expense pie breakdown charts.
- Currency preferences and net balance calculation.
- Spreadsheet goal tracking with timeline visualizer.

#### 👥 Social & Cloud Sync
- Real-time global and friends XP leaderboards powered by Firebase.
- In-app friend chat and milestone activity stream.
- Filtered out test/demo user accounts from leaderboards.

#### 🎨 Customization
- Added **Strawberry 🍓** theme preset with soft berry palette.
- 25+ preset themes (Dark, Light, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Midnight, Matcha, Arcane, etc.).
- Custom theme creator with live CSS variable editor.
