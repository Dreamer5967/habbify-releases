# 🔒 Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

At **Habbify**, we are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using the Habbify desktop application and web platform.

---

## 1. 🛡️ Data Privacy First

Habbify is designed with a **privacy-first architecture**. 

- **Local Storage**: By default, your personal habits, gym plans, finance logs, routines, goals, and journal entries are stored locally on your device in your local browser/Electron storage.
- **No Third-Party Tracking**: We do not use third-party tracking pixels, marketing cookies, or telemetry analytics scripts to track your personal browsing habits.

---

## 2. 👥 Optional Social Features & Cloud Sync

If you voluntarily create an account to use the **Social Leaderboard & Cloud Sync** features:

- **Account Information**: We store your chosen display username, avatar selection, level, and earned XP in Firebase Firestore.
- **Friends & Chat**: Friend requests and encrypted chat messages are processed through Firebase to enable real-time messaging between mutual friends.
- **No Personal Identifiable Info (PII) Required**: You do not need to provide real names, phone numbers, or private addresses to use Habbify.

---

## 3. 💾 Data Ownership & Export

You own 100% of your data. 

- You can reset or purge your local habit & app data at any time directly in **Settings > Danger Zone**.
- You can log out of your cloud account at any time to disconnect social sync.

---

## 4. 📜 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published in this repository with a revised "Last Updated" date.
